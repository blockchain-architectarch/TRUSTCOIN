05 — The G-Coefficient

Time is the only asset
you cannot buy.

Every holder receives a multiplier based on their entry date. Month 1 is ×10.00. Month 72 is ×0.20. The curve is linear, step −0.138 per month — no manipulation, no discretion.

The G-coefficient is cryptographically bound to a wallet address. It cannot be sold. It cannot be transferred. Any outgoing transaction resets G to zero — permanently, no recovery. This is the first loyalty metric in history that cannot be counterfeited.
